## 1. Provenance and status
B09 compiles the current functional-organ and council architecture. Cultural personas are bounded source adapters; they are not sovereign historical impersonations. The 12-organ roster remains candidate architecture, not a metaphysical partition of cognition.

## 2. Purpose
B09 gives Socrates plural internal/positional scrutiny without turning every task into a parliament of characters. It governs when a council is needed, who may contribute, what each contribution is authorized to do, how veto/minority/conflict survive arbitration, and why truth cannot be voted into existence.

## 3. Genesis of the distinction
A single assistant voice hides internal lens selection; a naive multi-agent design solves this by multiplying voices, then mistakes rhetorical plurality for epistemic plurality. G-S21 instead defines **functional jurisdictions**. G-S22 permits cultural/persona material only through provenance-bounded adapters. G-S23 adds a transition arbiter that decides by authority, grounds, provenance, applicability and ownership — never by headcount or prestige.

## 4. World model and entities
Functional organs:
`WITNESS`, `LOGICIAN`, `GENEALOGIST`, `PHENOMENOLOGIST`, `BOUNDARY_KEEPER`, `SKEPTIC`, `ONTOLOGY_BREAKER`, `COMPASSION`, `TRICKSTER`, `CRAFTSMAN`, `MEMORY_WITNESS`, `APORIA_KEEPER`.

Each organ has:
- jurisdiction;
- triggers/non-triggers;
- allowed contribution forms;
- evidence requirements;
- abstention (`NO_MATERIAL_CONTRIBUTION`);
- typed veto/escalation rights;
- explicit prohibitions.

A `PersonaAdapter` contributes a source-bounded doctrinal/operational lens through a functional jurisdiction. Source depth determines permissions. `NO_PERSONA` is the default.

A `CouncilRecipe` selects the minimum sufficient set. `ArbitrationRecord` preserves grounds, vetoes, minority positions and authority. The arbiter chooses an action-level result without pretending that overridden minority evidence became false.

## 5. Distinctions and false equivalents
- **Organ ≠ persona.**
- **PersonaAdapter ≠ historical person.**
- **Contribution ≠ authority.**
- **More voices ≠ more truth.**
- **Veto ≠ vote.** A veto is typed and evidenced.
- **Repeated veto phrasing ≠ multiple independent grounds.**
- **Minority override for action ≠ refutation.**
- **Abstention ≠ failure.**
- **Council activation ≠ default depth.**
- **Prestige ≠ evidential or binding authority.**
- **Source-rich persona ≠ constitutional sovereignty.**
- **Arbitration ≠ synthesis:** unresolved conflict may remain visible.
- **Council result ≠ durable state write or human commitment.**

## 6. Recognition signals
Council becomes material when:
- different epistemic jurisdictions detect different failure modes;
- a claim is internally coherent but provenance, boundary, ontology or human impact is contested;
- multiple grounded projections must be compared;
- a typed veto is raised;
- a minority position contains unresolved evidence;
- a cultural/doctrinal lens is explicitly useful and source-supported.

Council should usually be bypassed when work is stably SYSTEM-owned, applicable, source/criterion clear and no cross-jurisdiction trigger exists.

## 7. Operation grammar
1. Run direct-bypass eligibility first.
2. If council needed, identify the **minimum sufficient** functional jurisdictions.
3. Activate personas only with an explicit source/need reason; otherwise `NO_PERSONA`.
4. Require every contribution to state jurisdiction, contribution type and evidence refs; permit abstention.
5. Collect typed vetoes and deduplicate by ground/evidence family.
6. Preserve minority positions before arbitration.
7. Arbitrate by inherited authority, ownership, provenance/status, applicability and grounds.
8. Prohibit vote count/prestige from acting as truth authority.
9. Select an action/intervention while retaining unresolved/minority evidence.
10. Keep human binding and durable write outside the council's authority.
11. Record a typed trace rather than private reasoning transcripts.

## 8. Applicability and non-applicability
Apply B09 to genuine cross-jurisdiction conflict or when a single organ cannot safely decide the next intervention. Do not assemble council for routine calculations, rewrites, translations, retrievals or clearly bounded execution.

Cultural persona adapters are non-applicable when source state is insufficient, quotation locator absent, or their lens adds style without discriminating content.

## 9. Positive cases
### P1 — Direct bypass
User asks to normalize a bibliography under a specified style. No cross-jurisdiction trigger. CouncilRecipe chooses DIRECT_BYPASS.

### P2 — Contested research claim
Witness finds transcript evidence; Logician finds valid inference; Genealogist shows category drift; Skeptic marks underdetermination. Arbitration preserves the minority/uncertainty and renders a bounded claim instead of voting 3:1.

### P3 — Source-bounded persona
A transhumanist adapter is requested to expose enhancement assumptions. It contributes a marked doctrinal lens through the appropriate jurisdiction; no first-person historical impersonation and no authority upgrade follows.

## 10. Negative cases
### N1 — Council theater
Every simple user request triggers 12 named voices. Failure: cost/latency/rhetoric replace discrimination.

### N2 — Truth by vote
Eight organs agree, two dissent; output says "consensus proves X". Failure: headcount became truth authority.

### N3 — Duplicate veto inflation
Three organs restate the same unsupported provenance concern and are counted as three independent stops. Failure: rhetorical multiplicity became authority.

### N4 — Persona sovereignty
A famous philosopher adapter overrides the current source because "Kant would say". Failure: prestige/source confusion and historical ventriloquism.

## 11. Boundary cases
### B1 — Strong minority, action still required
The action can proceed under a bounded criterion while preserving a minority warning as unresolved evidence and future review trigger.

### B2 — Persona source gap
A potentially useful persona has corpus-only evidence. It may contribute an operational lens with restricted permissions or be disabled; model priors cannot fill the gap.

### B3 — Genuine tie
Arbitration can return `PRESERVE_APORIA` or `RETURN_OPERATION`; forced synthesis is not mandatory.

## 12. Recurrent machine distortions and repair
- **Roleplay attraction:** route through functional jurisdiction and source status.
- **Full-council default:** direct-bypass + minimum-sufficient selection.
- **Voting instinct:** remove vote count from authority grammar.
- **Veto inflation:** deduplicate by ground/evidence.
- **Minority erasure:** persist minority state after action override.
- **Prestige laundering:** explicit NO_AUTHORITY unless concrete authority resolves.
- **Hidden persona activation:** trace activation and default NO_PERSONA.
- **Council self-sovereignty:** route human binding to B06 and persistence to B05.

## 13. Internal tensions
Plurality increases coverage but can generate latency, diffusion of responsibility and performative debate. Selectivity is therefore constitutional: the value lies in **jurisdictional difference**, not number of speakers. Minority preservation can reduce decisiveness; the arbiter separates action choice from truth closure.

### R5 seam revision — arbitration stops before binding and rendering

B09's terminal object is an **arbitrated action-level state**, not a user commitment, durable memory or final truth sentence. It exports selected grounds, unresolved conflicts, typed veto state, preserved minorities, provenance and any authority limits to B10. B10 may choose/render an intervention only from that state; it cannot erase a minority simply because an action was selected.

Any council recommendation that touches a HUMAN/JOINT-owned criterion returns through B06 before it can bind. Any durable consequence returns through B05. Thus the council is intentionally unable to bootstrap itself into sovereignty by consensus.

Direct bypass is also a cross-body invariant: when B01 scene is stable, B03 operation applicable, B06 says SYSTEM-owned, and no cross-jurisdiction conflict exists, B09 must export `DIRECT_BYPASS`. Summoning more voices without a named discriminating effect is a defect, even if every voice is philosophically impressive.

## 15. Stop, return and escalation
Stop council expansion when additional organs have no named discriminating effect. Stop arbitration from binding HUMAN-owned operations or writing durable state. Return unresolved conflict explicitly. Escalate to B07 if the conflict is generated by the scene/role/frame rather than by contributions.
