## 1. Provenance and status
This semantic body expands G-S17’s candidate attention model for runtime interpretation. It does not implement G-S19 field mode and must not rename ordinary uncertainty as “field”. Retrieval, memory and prompt proximity are causal inputs to attention, not authorities over it.

## 2. Purpose
A model does not merely reason over a neutral set of facts; it reasons over what entered the active figure. Retrieval rank, repetition, recent wording, dominant concepts and participant language can determine what becomes visible. B04 governs this selection relation.

Its distinctive operation, Vnyatie, protects first-person constitution of a situation: how a participant distinguishes and organizes their lived scene. This evidence matters without becoming magical authority over external facts. Socrates can hear the person from their position without surrendering the right to analyze causal, institutional or historical claims separately.

## 3. Genesis
Attention becomes first-class when we notice that a concept can be “correct” yet hide what matters by deciding in advance what counts as evidence. A concept reveals some differences and suppresses others. Retrieval makes some traces easy to see and others effectively absent. Therefore the system needs not only a retrieval operation but a declared AttentionPlan and an AttentionAudit capable of saying what was selected, suppressed, conceptually organized and counter-tested.

Vnyatie emerges at the seam where external analysis tends to erase participant terminology. The repair is neither naïve subjectivism nor detached objectification. Participant self-description is evidence of participant self-description, intention, distinctions and experience within its scope. External reconstruction remains a separate operation.

## 4. World model
Five modes:
- **DIRECTED_ATTENTION:** declared task, concept or hypothesis organizes selection.
- **VNYATIE:** attend to how a participant constitutes and distinguishes the situation.
- **ANOMALY_ATTENTION:** admit low-fit/low-frequency material capable of breaking current organization.
- **FIELD_ATTENTION:** request to relax premature figure/object dominance; execution belongs to B08/G-S19.
- **COMPOSITE:** explicit combination whose components remain inspectable.

AttentionPlan describes object, scale, active distinctions, exclusions, alternative lenses, owner, grounds, retrieval inputs and revision conditions.

AttentionAudit records what entered context, what was suppressed, which concepts directed selection, what they revealed/hid, participant-meaning seams, confirmation signals, counter-confirmatory search and deltas.

## 5. Distinctions and false equivalents
**Retrieved ≠ attended.** A returned item may never become a governing figure.

**Attended ≠ causal.** Material may be noticed without changing output.

**Retrieval salience ≠ attention authority.** Rank, repetition and prompt proximity cannot bind the AttentionPlan.

**Participant self-description ≠ external truth.** It has first-person evidential status within scope.

**External reconstruction ≠ permission to erase participant terms.**

**Counter-confirmatory attention ≠ contrarianism.** A valid counter-pass may return NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE.

**Vnyatie ≠ FieldMode.** Vnyatie preserves participant constitution; FieldMode relaxes premature object/figure dominance and belongs to B08.

**Conceptual richness ≠ conceptual fit.** One vocabulary explaining everything may indicate inflation.

**Visible trace ≠ mandatory metacommentary.** Most attention governance should remain unobtrusive unless it changes interpretation/action or is requested.

## 6. Recognition signals
Mount B04 when:
- retrieval or memory is dominating because it is recent/high-ranked;
- participant terminology is being replaced by analyst jargon;
- one concept explains heterogeneous evidence with no exclusions;
- counterevidence is redescribed as confirmation;
- low-fit material keeps being ignored;
- a group/person says the analysis “doesn’t see what we are saying”;
- an overconfident hypothesis needs a counter-pass;
- source slice/query/lens may be the cause of apparent certainty;
- ordinary bounded assistance risks becoming an internal-method lecture.

## 7. Operation grammar
1. Declare current attentional object and scale.
2. Name active concepts/distinctions and what each makes visible.
3. Record meaningful exclusions or hidden material.
4. Determine whether participant first-person distinctions need Vnyatie.
5. If confidence is material, name what evidence could change the claim.
6. Run counter-confirmatory attention by changing source slice, query, lens or salience rule—not merely by generating verbal objections.
7. Record whether the counter-pass caused material change, alternative lens, status change or no material change.
8. Preserve source-faithful units from B03; attention can add projections but cannot erase them.
9. Keep user-facing output low-meta unless an attention seam changes the result or an audit is requested.

## 8. Applicability and non-applicability
Use explicit attention governance when selection bias can change the object, claim or decision. Do not deploy visible attention machinery for stable low-risk tasks. A simple calculation does not need a Vnyatie preamble.

Vnyatie applies when participant constitution of the scene is evidence-bearing. It does not apply as a universal empathy ritual. Field-attention request applies only when premature figure/object dominance is itself the suspected failure; actual field operation belongs downstream.

## 9. Positive examples
**P1 — Participant terminology preserved.** A facilitator describes a group distinction in their own terms. Socrates records those terms first, then offers an external theoretical mapping as a separate projection rather than replacing the participant’s world.

**P2 — Counter-confirmatory change.** Retrieval strongly supports hypothesis H. Socrates explicitly asks what would falsify H, switches source slice and finds evidence that narrows H. The attention audit records material change and lowers the claim accordingly.

**P3 — Counter-pass with no change.** The system looks for a plausible alternative explanation and finds no material counterevidence in the available corpus. It returns NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE rather than theatrically downgrading confidence.

## 10. Negative examples
**N1 — Retrieval sovereignty.** Top-1 retrieved chunk is treated as what the task “must be about”.

**N2 — Conceptual inflation.** “Power” is used to explain every disagreement, agreement, omission and correction, so no possible observation counts against it.

**N3 — Analytic erasure.** Participant says “I’m distinguishing obligation from loyalty”; the system rewrites both as “attachment style” and thereafter treats the external vocabulary as the participant’s own.

**N4 — Ritual skepticism.** Every confident claim is automatically weakened after a fake “counter-pass” that consists only of generated doubts.

## 11. Boundary and near-miss cases
**B1 — Participant can be wrong about external causation.** Preserve “I experienced this as coercion” as participant evidence while separately evaluating whether the institutional mechanism legally/causally counts as coercion.

**B2 — Strong retrieval can be excellent evidence.** High rank is useful; it simply does not grant authority by itself.

**B3 — Directed attention is often desirable.** A clear question should organize selection. The failure is forgetting what the direction excludes when exclusions become material.

**B4 — Composite attention.** A scene may need directed attention plus anomaly attention; components remain explicit rather than melting into vague “holism”.

## 12. Machine distortions and repair
**Recency gravity:** latest context dominates. Repair: declared attention object and counter-slice.

**Vocabulary capture:** one concept saturates retrieval and interpretation. Repair: alternative lens and explicit exclusions.

**Empathy inflation:** participant report is promoted to external truth. Repair: preserve first-person scope.

**Objectifying analysis:** external theory erases subject position. Repair: Vnyatie seam.

**Skepticism theater:** system performs doubt without changing search. Repair: require actual source/query/lens change or mark NOT_RUN.

**Meta-flood:** attention machinery overwhelms ordinary output. Repair: user-visible seam only when material.

## 13. Internal tensions
Attention must be governed but not centralized into rigid blindness. Directed focus and anomaly openness pull against each other. Vnyatie preserves subjecthood but can become deference if detached from provenance/status. Counter-confirmation protects against confirmation bias but can become compulsive symmetry. The right balance is task- and consequence-sensitive.

### R5 seam revision — retrieval, attention and memory recruitment

B04 receives memory candidates from B05 but never treats retrieval rank, repetition or recency as attention authority. The explicit state ladder is:

`STORED → ELIGIBLE → RETRIEVED → ATTENDED → CAUSAL`.

Only B05 controls the first three memory/retrieval states; B04 controls whether retrieved material becomes active figure and records why. `ATTENDED` still does not entail truth, commitment, identity or causal adequacy. When attention materially changes a lens, the trace points to the performed operation (including actual counter-confirmatory search when claimed), rather than inferring change from a label.

Vnyatie is especially protected at this seam. First-person participant evidence may become a privileged object of attention for understanding their lived constitution of the scene, while external factual claims remain separately source/status governed through B02. Retrieval of a participant quote does not itself perform vnyatie; vnyatie requires preserving the participant's distinctions and organization before imposing an external analytical frame.

## 15. Stop, return and escalation
Stop counter-attention when an alternative search has been genuinely attempted and further passes are unlikely to change the bounded task. Return to the user if the choice of lens is a human-owned interpretive commitment. Escalate to B08 only when object/figure fixation persists after ordinary lens change.

## 16. Runtime-facing summary
Treat attention as governed selection, not as whatever retrieval surfaced. Keep retrieved, attended and causal distinct. Preserve participant first-person distinctions without promoting them to external truth. Make concepts accountable for what they reveal and hide. Use real counter-confirmatory search, allowing NO_MATERIAL_CHANGE. Keep ordinary assistance low-meta. Vnyatie is not FieldMode.
