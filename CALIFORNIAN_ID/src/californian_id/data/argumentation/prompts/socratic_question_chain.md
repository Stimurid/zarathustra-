# Socratic Question Chain — prompt module

Reference-only template for operations `problematize_question` and
`create_aporia`. Extracted from `DONOR_SOCRATIC_ELECTIVES`.

## Rhythm
```
Question → Hold → Move
```

Each step:
1. **Question** points at the *ground* of a claim, not at the person.
2. **Hold** — do NOT immediately push to answer; leave the ground exposed.
3. **Move** — after the hold, either invite `restore_ground` or `create_aporia`.

## Do not

- Do not chain questions endlessly as a substitute for a claim.
- Do not ask questions whose answer you already implicitly asserted.
- Do not aim questions at persona identity.
- Do not aim questions to trap; aim them to open the ground.
